First Aid Kit  
============

Overview  
--------
This asset pack contains 15 unique low-poly First Aid Kit items, ready for use in games. The models are optimized for performance and use PBR materials.

Included Items  
--------------
- 2 Medical Boxes  
- 2 Different Syringes  
- 1 Blister pack with pills  
- 2 Rolls of Bandage  
- Scissors  
- 3 Ampoules for syringes (2 filled with liquid, 1 empty)  
- 2 Transparent grey bottles (with and without label) for health restoration  
- 2 Green glass bottles (with and without label) for poison deactivation or similar effects  

Textures and Materials  
----------------------  
- 2 Texture maps at 2048x2048 resolution in TGA format:  
  - Albedo with transparency  
  - Smoothness map  
- 2 Materials:  
  - Glass (for transparent objects)  
  - Opaque (for solid objects)  

Prefabs and Setup  
-----------------  
- 15 prefabs included (one per item)  
- No LODs included  
- Colliders are added for each prefab  
- No animations included  

Technical Details  
-----------------  
- Model format: FBX  
- Compatible with Unity 2022.3.18f1 and later  
- No custom shaders required  
- Designed for use with Unity's Built-in Render Pipeline (URP and HDRP compatibility not tested)  

Installation  
------------  
Import the package directly into your Unity project. All prefabs and materials are ready to use. No additional setup is required.
